Keep the judgment human.

Own Words is not a one-click paraphraser. It proposes lexical and structural alternatives, marks every automated change, and leaves the decision to you. The final language should be yours, not merely a second machine's version.

Everything happens in this page. There is no account, server-side rewrite, database, or AI API call containing your prose. Reload the page and your text is gone.

Changing prose can change statistical signals in machine-generated text, but Own Words does not promise to defeat any particular watermark or detector.
