A higher score means more of the token path and local token neighborhoods have changed. It does not establish authorship and does not prove that any particular provenance signal has been removed.

Keep the judgment human.

Own Words is not a one-click paraphraser. It proposes lexical and structural alternatives, marks every automated change, and leaves the decision to you. The final language should be yours, not merely a second machine's version.

Everything happens in this page. There is no account, server-side rewrite, database, or AI API call containing your prose. Text processing is capped at 2,500 words and 20,000 characters per passage.

Rewrite depth measures linguistic re-expression using model-neutral token comparisons. It is descriptive, not a claim to defeat any particular watermark or detector.
