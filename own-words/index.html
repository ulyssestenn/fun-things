Keep the judgment human.

Own Words is deliberately not a one-click paraphraser. It proposes small lexical and phrase-level changes, shows every one, and lets you cycle or reject them. The point is to make the final language yours, not to replace one machine voice with another.

Your text is processed entirely in this page. There is no account, server-side rewrite, analytics payload containing your prose, or AI API call. Reload the page and the text is gone.

Changing prose can also change statistical signals embedded in machine-generated text, but this tool does not claim to remove or defeat any particular watermark or detector. Those systems differ, and their behavior can change.
